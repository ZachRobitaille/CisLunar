UNDERSTAND

Solar illumination and
direct-to-Earth communications

The Sun shapes solar power availability and thermal conditions. Earth must be visible for a direct link. Near the south pole, terrain and timing affect both.

☼

Sun: power and temperature

Solar elevation, sunlight duration and shadows affect solar power potential. Illumination alone does not determine equipment power output or temperature.

◎

Earth: direct communication windows

Earth’s position relative to the local horizon determines geometric visibility windows. Antennas, power and ground-station availability still need to be checked.

At the south pole, the Sun stays low and shadows can be long. NASA ↗
