DESIGN GOALS · NOT YET VALIDATED

Proposed features

Select and compare

Reference sites or custom coordinates; comparison of multiple locations or dates.

Explore time

Date and time, changing conditions, and time until sunset or Earthset.

Read the conditions

Sun and Earth positions, solar elevation, sunlight duration and direct visibility windows.

2D maps, 3D views and timelines are possible presentation approaches. The final format, features and performance remain to be confirmed.

Data and accuracy: what needs validation

Ephemerides, such as JPL Horizons or precomputed datasets, must be combined with local terrain, such as LOLA data. Reference frames, time, libration and terrain resolution will need consistent treatment.

Without Earth-like atmospheric refraction, visibility is limited by terrain and lunar curvature. Solar elevation alone does not determine electrical output, and Earth visibility does not guarantee a radio link.
